What does Dark Predictions actually include?
Four universes in one place: original predictions (with photos and videos), original music tracks, applications I build, and original paintings. VIP members also get a private direct line to message me.
How do I access the content after subscribing?
As soon as your Stripe payment goes through, your membership activates. Log in with the account you created and the content unlocks instantly across the whole site.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Memberships are month-to-month with no contract. Cancel anytime from your Stripe billing portal — your access stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for.
What's the difference between Basic, Gold, and VIP?
Basic gives you monthly uncensored predictions videos, all my music free to download, and 50% off personal predictions. Gold adds monthly crypto & stock predictions, free access to my apps, and early access to new music. VIP unlocks everything in Basic and Gold, plus private messaging with me, gold & silver predictions, extra crypto & stock predictions, and your name or business featured in a future video.
How does the VIP private contact work?
VIP members get access to a private messaging space. Send me messages directly through the site — I read every one and reply personally.
How often is new content posted?
Predictions are posted regularly. Music tracks, apps and paintings are added as I create them — members get them as they drop, and Gold/VIP members get early access.
Is my payment secure?
Yes. All transactions are processed by Stripe, a trusted global payment provider. Your card details never pass through this website.
Can I upgrade or downgrade later?
Anytime. Many members start on Basic to try it out and move up to Gold or VIP once they're comfortable.
